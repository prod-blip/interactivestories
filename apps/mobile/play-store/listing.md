## Short description

## Full description

Step into four gentle, interactive story worlds made for children ages 4–8 and
the grown-ups beside them.

Moonlit Stories turns familiar fables into calm shared adventures. Read each
moment aloud, guide the characters through colourful 3D worlds, and pause to
talk about kindness, patience, persistence, and clever thinking.

Explore these stories:

- The Mouse and the Lion
- The Crow and the Pitcher
- The Tortoise and the Rabbit
- The Tortoise and the Tiger

Each tale combines simple touch controls, character moments, gentle sound, and
meaningful interaction. Every movement helps the story unfold—without ads,
accounts, or distracting notifications.

Designed for co-play:

- Read-aloud story moments
- Simple child-friendly interaction
- Two complete stories included
- Two more stories available as one permanent collection purchase
- No advertising or recurring subscription
- Works offline after installation

Settle in together, let your child lead, and make story time something you can
explore side by side.
